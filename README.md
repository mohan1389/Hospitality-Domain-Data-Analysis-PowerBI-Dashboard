# Hospitality-Domain-Data-Analysis-PowerBI-Dashboard

1. Hotel Booking & Revenue Analysis Dashboard: 
Developed an interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard to analyze hotel revenue, occupancy, booking trends, and customer performance for AtliQ Grands. The project delivers actionable business insights to support strategic decision-making, improve operational efficiency, and help regain market share in the hospitality industry.

2. PURPOSE: 
Many hotels struggle with declining revenue, low occupancy, and poor decision-making due to the lack of data-driven insights. This project helps analyze booking patterns, customer trends, and revenue performance to identify business gaps and improve strategic planning. The dashboard enables hotel management to make informed decisions for increasing profitability and market share.

3. TECH STACK: 
   
    • 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.

    • 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
   
    • 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
   
    • 📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

4. DATA SOURCE: AtliQ Grands owns multiple five-star hotels across India. They have been in the hospitality industry for the past 20 years. Due to strategic moves from other competitors and ineffective decision-making in management, AtliQ Grands are losing its market share and revenue in the luxury/business hotels category. As a strategic move, the managing director of AtliQ Grands wanted to incorporate “Business and Data Intelligence” to regain their market share and revenue.

5. BUSINESS PROBLEM: 
AtliQ Grands was facing a decline in revenue and market share due to increasing competition and ineffective decision-making in the hospitality industry. The company lacked a proper analytics system to monitor booking performance, occupancy trends, and customer behavior.

6. GOAL OF THE DASHBOARD: 
The goal of this dashboard was to provide data-driven insights into hotel performance, revenue trends, occupancy rates, and booking behavior. It helps management make strategic decisions to improve customer experience, optimize operations, and increase profitability.
